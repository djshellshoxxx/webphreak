Tone Calculator

Arrange any tone on this site, raw frequencies and silences into a sequence, with exact timing. Type a formula into the display or build it with the keys, then play it, save it, share it or download it as a WAV. Hover or focus any key for help.

Formula syntax

A formula is a list of things to play, one after another. +, commas, spaces and new lines all mean “then”. Anything after // on a line is a comment.

WriteMeaningExample
#411Key presses: each character is one press (DTMF keys by default)DT@2s 1s #411
@ timeHow long the tone before it sounds. Units ms or s; no unit means ms. Default 500 ms5@2s, 2600Hz@2ms, 1@80
time aloneSilence. You may add Si, or write Si@time, _@time or rest@time50ms, 1sSi, Si@250ms
KP, DT, [MF 1]Any catalog tone by its key label, alias, id or name. Use square brackets when the name has spaces or starts with a digitDialtone, [acts-quarter], [2600]
nHzA raw sine frequency, 1–20000 Hz (kHz also works)440Hz, 1.2kHz@100ms
&Stack: sound things together. Works with frequencies, single keys and steady tones697Hz&1209Hz (= DTMF 1), 1&5
%nRelative level (0–100) of one tone697Hz%60&1209Hz
N( … ) or ( … )xNRepeat a group N times (up to 10000)10(2600Hz@2ms 2ms), (1 50ms)x3
( … )@timeDefault tone length for everything in the group(KP 121 ST)@68ms
tone= gap= level=Settings for everything after them: default press length, automatic silence between tones, overall leveltone=70ms gap=70ms level=80%
keys=dtmf|mf|pulseWhat digits mean: keypad tones, MF trunk tones or rotary-dial clickskeys=mf KP 1212 ST
$name = …A variable, on its own line. Use it later as $name$num = 5551212 then DT@1s $num

Good to know

  • Tones that already have their own rhythm, such as busy (BT) or ringback, repeat to fill the length you give them, cutting the last cycle. Fixed sequences such as coin tones or SIT keep their own timing.
  • Stacked tones are mixed at equal level unless you use %. The loudest moment of the whole formula is scaled to −6 dBFS for playback and WAV export.
  • Every tone gets 4 ms fades (or half its length if shorter) to avoid clicks, inside its stated time.
  • Limits: 120 seconds and 20,000 steps per formula. Data bursts (SAME, MDC1200…) cannot be sequenced.
  • Saved formulas stay in this browser. 🔗 LINK puts the formula into the page address so you can share it; nothing is uploaded.

This tool arranges sounds for listening and audio files. It has no connection to any telephone line or radio. Historical trunk tones (2600 Hz, MF, coin tones) have no effect on modern networks.
