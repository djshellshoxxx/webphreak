Tone Calculator

Arrange any tone on this site, raw frequencies and silences into a sequence, with exact timing. Type a formula into the display or build it with the keys, then play it, save it, share it or download it as a WAV. Hover or focus any key for help, and see the reference below.

Formula reference

Steady catalog tones behave like frequencies. Repeating tones (busy BT, ringback RT, howler ROH) repeat to fill the length you give them. Fixed sequences (coins, SIT, pulse digits) keep their own timing. Data bursts (SAME, MDC1200…) cannot be used.

Dial strings

Text in double quotes is dialed like a stored phone number. Letters become keypad digits (2 ABC, 3 DEF, 4 GHI, 5 JKL, 6 MNO, 7 PQRS, 8 TUV, 9 WXYZ). , pauses (pause=, default 2 s), ; plays dial tone for wait= (default 2 s). Spaces, dashes, brackets, dots, + and / are ignored.

tone=90ms gap=90ms
"1-800-FLOWERS"
"9;5551212,,123#"
keys=pulse gap=700ms "CALL"
Variables, macros and sections

Define on its own line, use anywhere after it. Variables can hold frequencies, durations, keys, dial strings or whole sections.

$E = 329.63Hz
$num = 5551212
$beep($f, $len) = $f@$len 80ms     // a macro with parameters
$intro = (                          // a multi-line section
  DT@1s 300ms
  tone=80ms gap=80ms 411
)
$E@450ms $num $beep(1000Hz, 100ms) $intro x2
Loops 
for $f in 300Hz..1500Hz step 100Hz: $f@80ms 20ms
for $n in C4..C5: $n@1/8                  // notes step in semitones
for $t in 100ms..20ms step 20ms: (tone=$t 1234)
for $k in (1, 4, 7, *, 0, #): $k@150ms 150ms
for $o in (C4, C5): (for $s in (0, 4, 7): $o@1/16 )

The body is the rest of the line or a ( … ) group that may span lines. Up to 10,000 iterations.

Cadences

cadence(tone, on, off, on, off, …) writes an on/off rhythm: cadence(440Hz&480Hz, 2s, 4s)x3 is North American ringback; cadence(400Hz&450Hz, 400ms, 200ms, 400ms, 2s) the British double ring.

Editor features
  • Live check below the display; click an error to select the problem.
  • Autocomplete: type part of a tone, setting, ~modifier, :waveform, note or [catalog id; ↑/↓ then Enter or Tab inserts, Esc closes.
  • ✨ TIDY makes spacing and units consistent without changing the sound; comments are kept.
  • In plain English explains each step; the timeline shows overlapping parts in lanes and the step list shows the expanded formula.
  • Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter) plays, Esc stops. 💾 SAVE, export/import and 🔗 LINK keep and share formulas.
Rendering and limits
  • Playback and WAV use the same rendered samples (44.1 kHz WAV, 16-bit mono). The loudest moment is scaled to −6 dBFS.
  • Every tone fades in/out by 4 ms (or ramp=) inside its stated length to avoid clicks.
  • Limits: 120 s, 20,000 notes, 10,000 repeats or loop steps, 1–20000 Hz, tones of at least 1 ms.

This tool arranges sounds for listening and audio files. It has no connection to any telephone line or radio. Historical trunk tones (2600 Hz, MF, coin tones) have no effect on modern networks.

WebPhreak is a historical audio demonstration. All sound is generated locally in your browser; nothing dials, transmits or connects to a telephone or radio network. Values are labelled by evidence status; see History & Sources. Repository: djshellshoxxx/webphreak.
