Call Progress & Alerts

Tones that tell a caller what the network is doing. Continuous tones play while held; cadences start and stop with a tap; sequences play once.

History and help

Precise tone plan

Before the 1960s–70s, North American dial and busy tones varied by exchange. AT&T’s precise tone plan standardized combinations of four frequencies so that machines and people could recognize them reliably (Wikipedia: Precise tone plan; Call-progress tone).

SIT and call screening

SIT tones let automatic dialers detect intercepts. Some consumer products played a SIT at the start of a call to make autodialers hang up (Wikipedia: SIT).

The receiver off-hook howler is played at your chosen volume only; real exchanges raised its level. Recording-warning intervals vary by vendor.
